Local-first AI desktop app

OpenJarvis

A JARVIS-style AI assistant that lives entirely on your PC — chat, live TV guide, movies & shows, kids' apps, crypto tools, offline AI models, and offline Wikipedia. No subscriptions, no cloud required.

View on GitHub See what's inside

Everything in one window

Chat-first interface with a pill bar for every part of the system.

◈ JARVIS Chat

Chat page is home. Animated arc-reactor boot screen, mic hotkey (F9), voice, and an assistant that can actually run tools on your machine.

▦ TV Guide

Rogers-style guide grid merging US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia & NZ channels with real schedules, sports/kids sections, and click-to-play streams.

6 regions1–3 day EPGlive now-line

▶ Movies & Shows

Browse catalogs, pick episodes, choose resolution — "Play best" speed-tests sources and plays the fastest. Torrents download to an app folder and play when done.

🧒 Kids

YouTube Kids, Google Classroom, PBS/Nick Jr, plus bundled Minecraft (PortableMC) and Roblox launchers and a one-click Minecraft server for friends.

₿ Crypto

Exchanges, AI trading bots, market graphs, mining pools, faucets and wallets — all one click away.

⌘ Apps & Installers

Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, streaming devices and popular apps. The download arrow grabs the real installer and runs it automatically.

📚 Offline Wikipedia

Download a Kiwix ZIM and the assistant can search and read Wikipedia with zero internet — powered by libzim.

🧠 Offline models

Runs on local Ollama models — chat, coding, and game-modding models included, all private and offline-capable.

🛠 Installer

Windows installers with admin/UAC choice, mic/camera/screen permission consent, and global hotkeys — install once, it just works.

Under the hood

Python FastAPI backend + React frontend, served locally on port 8000.

OpenJarvis ├── Chat (JARVIS UI, voice, tools, MCP agents) ├── Guide → regional playlists + windowed XMLTV EPG ├── VOD → Stremio addons + libtorrent progressive playback ├── Wiki → offline Kiwix ZIM via libzim ├── Models → Ollama (qwen, gemma, coders — all local) └── Installers → auto-resolve, download, extract & launch

Open-source · runs 100% locally · your data never leaves your machine.
